================================================================================
4. Scaling notes
================================================================================

Interval sensitivity
  All daily figures scale linearly with metrics_export.interval_ms and
  hw_metrics_export.interval_ms. Doubling the interval halves all numbers.

Fleet sizing
  Multiply per-host figures by the number of streamers writing to the same
  ClickHouse instance. Example: 10 hosts × 50 MiB/day ≈ 500 MiB/day,
  ≈ 45 GiB over the 90-day TTL.

TTL caps total table size
  Every table has `TTL toDateTime(ts) + INTERVAL 90 DAY` (see
  metrics/clickhouse_schema.sql:121 et seq.). Steady-state table size is
  bounded at ~90× the per-day figure per host.

Compression behaviour at scale
  Numbers above are taken AFTER OPTIMIZE FINAL on small parts. As parts grow,
  ZSTD/LZ4 ratios typically improve modestly for narrow time-series columns
  (DoubleDelta on ts already amortizes; remaining columns are dominated by
  Float32 noise that does not compress strongly). Treat the per-row figures
  as a sound upper bound — real production numbers should be at or slightly
  below these.
